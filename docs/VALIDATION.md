# Validation report

Generated by `python -m engine.validate` over 20 synthetic datasets (seeds 0 to 19),
each with 200 growers over two seasons, with names written the way third parties write them (typos,
"Last, First", initials, "Farms"). Every figure compares the engine's output with the generator's
ground truth, through the same resolve, reconcile, and score path the product runs.

## Headline

- **False positives: 0 of 7,800** honest grower-seasons were labeled `review recommended`.
- **Replanting caught: 160 of 160 (100.0%)**
  of saved-seed grower-seasons where a conclusion is possible (acreage report linked, seed statement
  on file, dealer coverage at least 90%). Of all 200 saved-seed grower-seasons,
  80.0% met those conditions; the rest are labeled by what was missing, never guessed.
- **Record linking: 0 wrong links** out of 19,878 records linked
  (99.8% of 19,914 customer records linked; the rest wait for an analyst).
  0 of 560 elevator tickets from non-customers were linked to anyone.
- **Replant signature:** shown on 160 of 160 judgeable saved-seed
  grower-seasons and on 0 honest ones. It is context only and never changes a score.
- **What would clear this:** for 918 of 918 grower-seasons that were not
  `consistent`, adding exactly the acres the engine names makes the arithmetic consistent.

## Labels by case

| Case | consistent | insufficient data | review recommended | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| clean | 6760 | 0 | 0 | 6760 |
| split_brand | 322 | 78 | 0 | 400 |
| missing_dealer | 0 | 400 | 0 | 400 |
| neighbor_dealer | 0 | 240 | 0 | 240 |
| ghost_seed | 0 | 40 | 160 | 200 |

- **clean:** honest; every acre planted with this company's seed.
- **split_brand:** honest; part of the acreage planted with another company's seed.
- **missing_dealer:** honest; part of the seed bought from a dealer in their county that does not report.
- **neighbor_dealer:** honest; the same, but the non-reporting dealer is in a bordering county.
- **ghost_seed:** bought licensed seed one season, then planted saved seed on part of a similar acreage.

Honest growers without a seed statement, or near a non-reporting dealer, land in `insufficient data`
by design: their data cannot rule out an innocent explanation.

## Thresholds

| Setting | Value | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Unsupported share of declared acres treated as noise | 5% | engine/score.py |
| Unsupported share of delivered bushels treated as noise | 25% | engine/score.py |
| Dealer coverage needed before a review | 90% | engine/score.py |
| Yield ceiling | county mean + 3 standard deviations | engine/reconcile.py |
| Fuzzy name match | similarity at least 0.85 | engine/resolve.py |
| Replant signature | explained share falls 30%, acreage within 20% | engine/replant.py |

## Limits

This is synthetic data written by the same author as the engine, so it tests the logic, not the
world. Real records will contain patterns the generator does not. The next step is a pilot on one
company's historical data with known outcomes.

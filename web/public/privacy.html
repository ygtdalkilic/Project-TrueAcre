Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026 · Back to TrueAcre · Terms of Use

TrueAcre ("the Service") is operated by Yigit Dalkilic ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the Service handles, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to match exactly what the software does.

1. Who this covers

Two kinds of people: users, who sign in and work in a company workspace, and data subjects, the growers, dealers, and other people named in the records a company uploads. Companies that upload records are responsible for having a lawful basis to do so (see the Terms of Use).

2. Data about users

When you sign in with Google we receive and store your Google account identifier, email address, display name, and profile picture URL. We use them to create your account, show who you are to members of your company, and match invites sent to your email. We do not receive your Google password and we store no passwords.

We keep one cookie, a signed session cookie, so you stay signed in for up to seven days. It contains no tracking identifiers and is not shared with anyone. We use no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking of any kind.

3. Data uploaded by companies

A company uploads seven tables: growers, dealers, varieties, seed purchases, acreage reports, elevator receipts, and county yields, and optionally an eighth, seed statements (acres a grower reports planting with other seed). These may contain personal data about growers, such as names and farm numbers. This data is:

  • visible only to members of the company that uploaded it;
  • encrypted at rest with a key held separately from the database;
  • processed solely to compute the Fair-Play Score and its supporting evidence for that company;
  • never used to train models, sold, or shared between companies.

Growers who sign in. A company may invite a grower to see their own records. A grower user sees only the records the company holds about that one grower, the arithmetic behind each season's score, and can file a seed statement (acres planted with other seed). A filed statement is stored with the grower ID, season, crop, acres, who filed it, and when; these rows are not encrypted like uploaded tables, contain no name, and are visible to the company's staff. Every statement is recorded in the company's audit log.

4. Optional plain-English explanations

If a company has this feature enabled and a user clicks "Explain in plain English", the evidence for that one grower-season (the numbers and record names shown on screen) is sent to the configured language-model provider to write a summary. Nothing is sent unless a user clicks the button. The provider is shown next to the button. For the hosted Service the provider is Google (Gemini API), subject to Google's terms.

5. Where data is processed

The Service runs on Google Cloud (Cloud Run, United States) with the database hosted by Neon (United States). Both providers encrypt data in transit and at rest. Server logs kept by Google Cloud contain request metadata such as IP addresses and timestamps and are retained per Google Cloud's defaults.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Any member can remove or replace an uploaded table at any time. Removal is immediate and permanent.
  • A company admin can delete the company; all of its tables, invites, and memberships are deleted immediately.
  • Any user can delete their account from the company selection page. Companies where they were the only member are deleted with it.
  • Deleted data may persist in the database provider's backups for a limited period before it expires.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, export, or erase personal data, and to object to or restrict processing. Users can exercise access and erasure directly in the Service as described above. For anything else, or if you are a data subject named in a company's records, contact us at the address below; where the company controls that data we will pass your request to it.

8. Security

Sign-in through Google only, encrypted storage of uploaded data, TLS everywhere, strict browser security headers, rate limits, and automated vulnerability scanning of every software dependency. No system is perfectly secure; see the project's security policy for details and how to report a problem.

9. Children

The Service is a business tool and is not directed at children under 16.
